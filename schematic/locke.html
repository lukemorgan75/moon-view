Bodleian MS Locke c. 27, f. 88r · about 1691

The diagram he sent Locke

An adaptation of the printed transcription, not a facsimile of the handwriting. Time runs downward. The two halves are the prophecy, and the interpretation of that prophecy. The sheet itself carries no years.

Diagram The book Schematic Timeline Guide

The wording in the grid is the transcription Stephen D. Snobelen published with Newton Project Canada. ye is the, and wth is with. The later synopsis, in Yahuda MS 7.2a, is a fuller drawing of the same book and is the diagram on the front page. This sheet stops at the phrases in the cells. The dashed lines are his. The first solid line is Theodosius. Then Arcadius and Honorius. Then the universal bishopric. The last dashed line is the end of the 1,260 days. He does not date that line here. In other papers he tries 609 and 800, and from 800 the count reaches 2060 and may end later. The gold boxes and the three ranges are set on that sheet from the later papers. They are not lines of the 1691 copy.
