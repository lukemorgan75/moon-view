Yahuda Ms. 1 · Untitled treatise on Revelation

The guide he built into the treatise

Newton put the guide at the front. First, how to read the prophets. Then a list of what each figure means. The cards are that list. The name is the front of the card. Open it for his sentence, and for the same sentence in today’s words. Under the cards, the manuscripts from THEM00135 on. Open a manuscript, then a section, then a paragraph.

Diagram The book Locke Timeline Schematic Figures Treatise

The opening, the rules, and the figure cards are put into today’s words. The rest of the treatise is his sentences with the spelling and the old pronouns brought forward. It is not a rewrite. Where two copies agree, the fuller wording is shown once. Where they differ and he does not mark which is last, both are shown. An earlier list headed Prophetic figures is left out. The Definitions are the revised list.
