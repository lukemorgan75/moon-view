The first telling

By opening the seals

Revelation 1–11. He dates the writing to the end of Nero’s reign, before Jerusalem falls.

  1. 1
    Patmos

    Write the things which thou hast seen, and the things which are, and the things which shall be hereafter. The seven letters are seven states of one church, beginning as the book begins.

  2. 2
    Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum, Thyatira

    Ephesus runs through the four horsemen. Smyrna is the fifth seal. Pergamum is the sixth. Thyatira is the division at Constantine’s death, still inside the sixth head.

  3. 3
    Sardis, Philadelphia, Laodicea

    Sardis is the reunion under Constantius. Philadelphia and Laodicea have no year. He does not accuse Philadelphia. Laodicea is the church about to be spewed out, and the remnant the dragon goes to war with.

  4. 4
    A door opened in heaven

    The throne, and the worship of the first temple. The same door opens again at 11:19.

  5. 5
    The sealed book

    The Lamb takes the book out of the right hand of him that sat upon the throne. Written within and on the backside: his warrant for reading two texts as one time.

  6. 6
    Six seals

    White, red, black, pale, the souls under the altar, and the heaven departing as a scroll. The seals follow one another and do not overlap. The seventh seal is the next chapter.

  7. 7
    The winds held

    144,000 sealed out of the tribes, before the four winds blow. The palm-bearing multitude stands in this chapter. The box is set on the millennium day.

    Set on the millennium day
  8. 8
    Silence, then four trumpets

    Silence in heaven about half an hour: Theodosius, who dies in 395. Then hail and fire, the burning mountain, Wormwood, and the third part of the sun. Trumpet, thunder, and vial are one war. The thunders are sealed.

  9. 9
    Two woes

    The fifth trumpet opens the pit. The sixth looses the four angels at the Euphrates. He starts that plague at 1258 and does not close it. The seventh trumpet is not sounded in this chapter.

  10. 10
    The little book

    The chapter stands in the scroll between the sixth trumpet and the seventh. The box is set at the end of the 1,260.

    Mark of the end of the 1,260
  11. 11
    The witnesses, and the second door

    Measure the temple. The two witnesses prophesy, are killed, and rise. He had not seen the killing. Verse 15 is the eighth part of the seventh seal. Verse 19 opens the temple and shows the ark, and stays at the head of the other page.

    Verse 15 is the eighth part · The millennium day

The second telling

By eating the book

The same history, not a sequel. These chapters stand later in the scroll. He reads them beside the seals and the trumpets.

Lifted to the head of this page

The temple opened

Revelation 11:19. The temple of God was opened in heaven, and there was seen in his temple the ark of his testament. In the Observations, the interpretation begins with these words.

Revelation 10 is not set here. Eating the book is the warrant to prophesy again. The chapter itself marks the end of the 1,260.

  1. 12
    The woman and the dragon

    Travail and war in heaven with the fifth seal. The child caught up, and the dragon cast down, with Constantine. The flight, the flood, the wrath, and the war on the remnant with the four states inside the sixth head.

    Read beside the seals
  2. 13
    Two beasts

    The beast from the sea, wounded and then revived. The beast with two horns makes an image and enforces a mark. He sets this scene at the opening of the seventh seal.

  3. 14
    Sion, then the harvest

    The 144,000 on mount Sion stand with the opening of the woes. The harvest and the vintage are set on the millennium day.

    Set on the millennium day
  4. 15
    The vials given

    Those who had gotten the victory stand on the sea of glass. Seven angels receive seven vials. The pouring is the next chapter, one vial to each trumpet.

  5. 16
    Seven vials

    Earth, sea, rivers, sun, the seat of the beast, the Euphrates, and the air. The same seven wars as the trumpets. The frogs come after the sixth vial. He had not seen them.

  6. 17
    The whore and the heads

    Seven heads are seven kings. Five are fallen, and one is, spoken from the sixth head. The eighth is of the seven. The ten horns receive power as kings.

  7. 18
    Babylon fallen

    He had not seen this fall. The tenth part of the city, in chapter 11, is only the forerunner, between Babylon’s two falls.

  8. 19
    The Word of God makes war

    The rider on the white horse. The beast and the false prophet are taken. The box is set on the millennium day.

    Set on the millennium day
  9. 20
    The thousand years

    Yahuda MS 6 places the thousand years at the seventh trumpet. The judgment begins when that trumpet sounds. He gives the trumpet no year-length. The box is the millennium day.

    The millennium day
  10. 21
    A new heaven and a new earth

    The holy city. The saints reign. On the sheet he sent Locke, this is the last box. It sits with the millennium day.

    Set on the millennium day
  11. 22
    The attestation

    These sayings are faithful and true. He does not fold the closing words into a seal or a trumpet.

The boxes, set where he reads them

Out of the order of the chapters

A gold line above means he lifts the passage. The box is set again here, at the time he gives it. The three counts are the bounds he wrote. They are not a dated ruler, and they are not a new prophecy.

Eight successive parts

In the Observations of 1733, Part II, Chapter II, the time of the seventh seal is subdivided into eight successive parts by the silence in heaven for half an hour, and the sounding of seven trumpets successively. The eighth part is the seventh trumpet. The book has no eighth trumpet, and none is added here. That trumpet sounds to the battle of the great day of God Almighty.

  1. 1 Silence in heaven about the space of half an hour.
  2. 2 The first trumpet.
  3. 3 The second trumpet.
  4. 4 The third trumpet.
  5. 5 The fourth trumpet.
  6. 6 The fifth trumpet, the first woe.
  7. 7 The sixth trumpet, the second woe. He starts it at 1258 and does not close it.
  8. 8 The seventh trumpet. The eighth part. No year-length.

The last two stretches are drawn wide enough to read. In the count they are thirty years, then forty-five. Yahuda MS 7.3, a slip written after 1705. The time, times, and half a time did not commence before the year 800, in which the Pope’s supremacy commenced, nor after the reign of Gregory VII, which he dates 1084. They do not end before 2060, nor after the damaged figure printed as [2344], and from 800 the count may end later. The 1,290 days did not commence before 842, nor after that same reign. The difference between the 1,290 and the 1,335 days, he writes, is a part of the seven weeks. On the end of the 1,290 the slip says they do not begin before 2090 nor after 1374. Stephen D. Snobelen reads “begin” as an end, and 1374 as 2374. That reading is his. The line stays as written.

10
Revelation 10, the mark of the end of the 1,260

A mighty angel comes down, clothed with a cloud, a rainbow on his head, his face as the sun, his feet as pillars of fire. He stands on the sea and on the earth, and cries as a lion. Seven thunders utter their voices, and John is told to seal them up and write them not. The angel swears by him that lives for ever and ever that there should be time no longer, but in the days of the voice of the seventh angel, when he begins to sound, the mystery of God should be finished, as he has declared to his servants the prophets.

The Observations read the oath as falling after the seven thunders, which last to the end of the prophetic times. In the treatise, chapter 10 and the first thirteen verses of chapter 11 are the parenthesis between the sixth trumpet and the seventh. Take the book, and eat it, and prophesy again, is the warrant for the second telling. The box itself stands here.

Day
The millennium day

The millennium, or thousand years, which are with God but as a day. That is his phrase in the Observations, Part II, Chapter I, for the day of the Lord. The seventh trumpet, the eighth part of the seventh seal, sounds to the battle of the great day of God Almighty, whereby the kingdoms of this world become the kingdoms of the Lord and of his Christ. Yahuda MS 6 places the thousand years at that trumpet. He gives it no year-length.

  • 7 The palm-bearing multitude comes out of the great tribulation.
  • 11 The seventh angel sounds.
  • 14 The harvest and the vintage.
  • 19 The Word of God makes war. The beast and the false prophet are taken.
  • 20 The thousand years. The judgment begins when the trumpet sounds.
  • 21 A new heaven and a new earth. On the sheet he sent Locke, this is the last box.

What lines up

One book, read in synchronism

  • The seven seals are the seven heads, and they do not overlap.
  • Inside the seventh seal, the trumpets, the thunders, and the vials are one set of seven wars. The thunders have no words. John was told not to write them.
  • The 1,260 days are the witnesses in sackcloth, the woman in the wilderness, and the reign he gives the whore. They begin at the woe trumpets. They do not cover the four winds. Revelation 10 is the mark of their end.
  • The seventh seal is eight successive parts: the half hour of silence, and seven trumpets. The eighth part is the seventh trumpet. The millennium day is that trumpet. The 1,290 and the 1,335 are the longer counts on the same slip as 2060.
  • The two witnesses, on the synopsis, are the letters to Smyrna and Philadelphia. A draft on the Goths names the churches of the Visigoths and the Ostrogoths, and reads the fire from their mouth as war. Both readings are kept on the schematic.
